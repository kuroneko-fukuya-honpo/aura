# Contact

For bug reports, requests, or anything else about AURA, email us.

- **Developer**: Black Cat Fortune Works (Kuroneko Fukuya)
- **Email**: [kuroneko.fukuya@gmail.com](mailto:kuroneko.fukuya@gmail.com)

We accept messages in English or Japanese.

In the app, go to **Settings > Info & support > Contact**.

If you run a source (a news site or blog) and would like it listed in AURA, see [Listing your source](/sources).

## Reporting a bug

The following helps us reproduce the problem.

1. Your device and Android version
2. Your AURA version (Settings > Info & support, to the right of the **About the app** heading; it also appears at the bottom of the first Settings screen)
3. What you did, and what happened
4. The source URL (if it happens with a specific feed)

Turn on **Logs for bug reports** in the app (Settings > Advanced) and reproduce the problem to record fetching and full-text activity.
Tap "Copy logs" to paste them into your email. **Logs are never sent off your device automatically.**
