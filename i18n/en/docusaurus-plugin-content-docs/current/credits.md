# AURA — Credits

Last updated: 2026-10-30 / App: **AURA** (Android) / Provider: **Black Cat Fortune Works (Kuroneko Fukuya)**

This page lists the sounds, voices, and libraries AURA uses whose licenses require a credit. In the app, you can read the full list, which also includes fonts and more, in Settings > Info & support > Terms and privacy > Licenses.

Credits required by the licenses are shown in their original form, with an English explanation.

## Sound effects

AURA uses sound effects from OtoLogic for the sounds at the start of read-aloud (the news sound and the breaking-news sound).

- Sound effects: OtoLogic (https://otologic.jp) CC BY 4.0
- The length and volume of some sounds have been adjusted.

License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en). OtoLogic does not endorse AURA.

## Audio briefing voices

The voices of the Japanese audio briefing of AURA Brief use VOICEVOX voice libraries.

- VOICEVOX:猫使アル・VOICEVOX:猫使ビィ (voice libraries from the Nekotsuka project)
- AURA does not use or distribute the characters' official images, and does not use the voices it creates to train AI.

## Japanese voice (AI voice)

- A voice created from koniwa (tnc) audiobook recordings. Audio: テレビ西日本 (TV Nishinippon) ([CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/deed.en)). The texts read aloud are works whose copyright has expired.
