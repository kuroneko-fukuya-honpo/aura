# Read and save

## Switch views

At the top left of the list, under “AURA,” tap **All articles ▾** (the name of the current view) to switch.

- **All articles**
- **Unread articles** (the default)
- **Unread articles (with full text)** — Only unread articles you can read offline
- **All articles (with full text)**
- **Saved articles**
- **Reading history**

AURA remembers your choice, so the next time you open it, you start where you left off.

## When articles are marked as read

- When you open an article (including an article you stop on while swiping left or right in the article screen)
- When Settings > Read & listen > **Open in browser** is on: when you tap it in the list

**Scrolling the list doesn't mark articles as read.**
Prefetching (keeping articles for offline reading) never changes read status.

## Mark many as read

Tap ✓✓ at the top right. Choose the **Range to mark as read**, then confirm.

- **Where** — The current view (shown by folder or feed name) / **All folders**
- **Age** — **All** / **1 day or older** / **1 week or older** / **1 month or older**

Check the count, then tap **Mark as read**. For a few seconds afterward, **Undo** also appears.

## Save

Keep articles to read later. **Saved articles aren't removed when AURA cleans up old articles to save space.**

- **Touch and hold** an article in the list (touch and hold again to remove it from saved)
- In single-column layouts (List and Large cards), **swipe right** on an article (swipe left to mark it read or unread)
- Tap **⋮** on an article > **Save**
- Tap the bookmark icon at the top of the article screen

## Old article cleanup

To keep the article count from growing too large, AURA automatically removes old articles from each source, oldest first, so everything fits within Settings > Offline & storage > **Amount to keep on device**.
Saved articles stay.

Once an article is removed, AURA **doesn't fetch it again**, even if the source still lists it.
Fetching it again would turn a read article back into unread.

:::info AURA Plus — Clean up read articles
With **[AURA Plus](/plus)**, go to Settings > Offline & storage > **Free up space** > **Clean up read articles**. Articles you've read are removed from the list at refresh after the number of days you choose (3, 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days). They stay in your reading history. Saved articles aren't removed.
:::

:::info AURA Plus — Filters
With **[AURA Plus](/plus)**, use Settings > Collect > **Filters** to hide articles that contain certain words, or pin them to the top.
:::
