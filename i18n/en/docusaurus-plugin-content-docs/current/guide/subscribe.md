# Add a feed

Everything starts from one screen: **Add a feed**. You can open it in any of these ways:

![Add a feed](/img/guide/en/subscribe-add.jpg)

- In the article list, tap **⋮** at the top right > **Add a feed**
- In **Manage feeds** (list **⋮** > **Manage feeds**), tap the round **+** at the bottom right
- When you have no articles yet, tap **Add a feed** on the empty screen

At the top is a search box (**Outlet name, URL, or keyword**). As you type, AURA shows matching outlets and a shortcut to look for a feed at that URL or to collect that word as a keyword.
Below it, **Ways to find feeds** shows four options.

| Way | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Source directory | Choose from the sources built into the app, by country or region and topic |
| Keyword | Collect articles by word from Google News |
| Enter a URL | Subscribe from a site URL or an RSS / Atom URL |
| From a file | Import an OPML file |

Further down, **English outlets by topic** takes you straight to the outlets for a topic, and **Popular picks** shows a few outlets you haven't subscribed to yet (**See all** opens **Source directory**).

## The first time you open AURA

**Start by adding a feed** offers a few English outlets: NPR News, BBC News, Ars Technica, The Verge, and NASA. None are selected at first, so tap the ones you want.
You can also enter a word under **Follow keywords (Free allows 3)**.
Then tap **Start AURA with N feeds**, or **Start without adding feeds**. You can add feeds anytime later with **Add a feed**.

## From an RSS or Atom URL

Tap **Enter a URL**, enter the URL, and tap **Find** (while the box is empty, the button is **Paste**). A site URL or an RSS URL both work: AURA finds the feed for you.
While it looks, you see “Looking for a feed… (up to 25 seconds).”

![Enter a URL (a feed was found)](/img/guide/en/subscribe-url.jpg)

- If it finds one, you see **Found a feed.** If it finds more than one, choose which one to subscribe to
- **Recent headlines** shows what the feed has been sending
- Choose the folder under **Add to folder** at the bottom, then tap **Add to …**

Older sources that start with `http://` work too (if `https://` connects, AURA tries it first and switches to it).

To hide list thumbnails and the image at the top of articles for a feed, turn off **Show article images** in its settings sheet after you add it (see [Folders](/guide/folders)).

## From a keyword

Tap **Keyword** to open **Collect by keyword**. Enter a word, choose the folder under **Add to folder** at the bottom (by default, a folder named **Keyword**), and tap **Collect “…”**. AURA subscribes to the Google News search results for that word.
Separate words with a space to get only articles that contain all of them.
In the list and the drawer, keyword feeds have a keyword mark, so you can tell them apart from URL feeds.

![Collect by keyword](/img/guide/en/subscribe-keyword.jpg)

You can also open **Collect by keyword** from **Search** (🔍 at the top of the list): in the results, tap **Subscribe to this word**.

Keywords use Google News for your country. The chip below the box shows the country and language. You can change the default in Settings > Collect > **News edition for keywords**.

### Where to search

Google News is always included. When the edition is in English, you can also choose these places (all are off by default):

- **Medium** — collected by tag: your words become a tag (spaces → hyphens)
- **DEV** — collected by tag, the same way
- **Hacker News** — stories whose title contains your words

With the Japan edition (Japanese), the places you can choose are note, Zenn, and Qiita instead.

Tap **Preview** to see which articles are found before you choose. Places you don't choose aren't fetched.

:::info AURA Plus
Free accounts can have **up to 3 keyword feeds**. The 4th and later are an **[AURA Plus](/plus)** feature.
If you have 4 or more on a free account, AURA refreshes only your 3 oldest. The rest are paused, not deleted.
Searching Google News in other countries (**Other countries**) and **World view** (how a word is covered in other countries, translated for you) are also AURA Plus features.
:::

## Google News topics

In **Source directory**, on the **English** tab, **To read many at once** has **Google News topics**: the official topic feeds from Google News (top stories, world, business, technology, and more).
Tap one to subscribe. Feeds you already have show **Subscribed**.

![Source directory > English > United States (Google News topics at the top)](/img/guide/en/subscribe-google.jpg)

## Source directory

If you don't want to look up URLs, tap **Source directory**. Switch between **English** and **Other languages** at the top, choose a country or region, then choose a topic and an outlet.
The directory is built into the app, so choosing doesn't send any requests.

In a topic's outlet list, check the outlets you want, then tap **Add N to …** at the bottom.
**Select all** (checks every outlet in the list that you haven't subscribed to) is an **[AURA Plus](/plus)** feature.

Some sources show **Not sent to AI**. By agreement with the publisher, articles from these outlets are never sent to AI or used to train AI. They are also left out of audio briefings and deep dives in reports. You can still read them, have them read aloud, and count them on your device.

If you run a source and want it listed in the directory, see [Listing your source](/sources).

## After you add a feed

Before adding, choose where it goes under **Add to folder** at the bottom of the screen. Tap an existing folder, or tap **+ New folder** and enter a name.
AURA suggests one for you: the folder you came from, a folder whose name matches the topic, or a new folder named after the topic. If none of these fit, it goes to **Uncategorized**.

![After adding](/img/guide/en/subscribe-added.jpg)

Then tap **Add to …**. AURA starts fetching the first articles. You can switch to another screen while this finishes.
On the screen that follows (“Added N to …”), tap **Open the … list** to read, or **Keep adding** to add more.
Under **Next**, you can also **See more outlets in the same topic** or **Collect by keyword**.

## From text you select in an article

Touch & hold a word in the article text to select it. **Add to keywords** appears in the menu.

![The menu when you select text in an article](/img/guide/en/subscribe-select.jpg)

Tap it to open **Collect by keyword** with the text you selected already in the box. Fix it if needed, choose the folder under **Add to folder**, then tap **Collect “…”**.

- Spaces and line breaks around the selected text, and quotation marks at both ends, are removed
- If you already have the same keyword feed, AURA shows ““…” is already registered” instead of opening the screen
- You can't add text that is only spaces or longer than 50 characters

The keyword feed limit (up to 3 on a free account, unlimited with **[AURA Plus](/plus)**) is the same as in “From a keyword” above.
If you already have 3 on a free account, you see AURA Plus information instead of **Collect by keyword**.

## From text you select in other apps

When you select text in Chrome and other apps, the menu (inside ⋮) also shows **Add to AURA keywords**.

Tap it, and AURA opens **Collect by keyword** with the text you selected already in the box. Choose the folder and tap **Collect “…”**.
Your selected text in the other app isn't changed.

To remove it from the menu, turn off **Show “Add to AURA keywords”** in Settings > Collect (on by default).

## Subscribe with Share from other apps (YouTube and more)

In your browser or the YouTube app, tap **Share** > **Subscribe with AURA** on the page you're viewing. AURA opens **Enter a URL** with what you shared and starts looking for a feed right away.

![A shared URL (a YouTube channel) after AURA found its feed](/img/guide/en/subscribe-share.jpg)

- **YouTube** — Share a video, a channel (including `@name` URLs), or a playlist page to subscribe to that channel's or playlist's RSS feed
- **Other sites** — AURA looks for an RSS feed in the page. If it finds more than one, choose which one to subscribe to

When AURA shows **Found a feed.**, check **Recent headlines**, choose the folder under **Add to folder**, and tap **Add to …**.
If you see “Couldn't find a feed.”, tap **Search the source directory by site name**.

Sharing and entering a URL in **Enter a URL** look for the feed in the same way.

## Import and export with OPML

In **Manage feeds**, tap **⋮** > **Import OPML** or **Export OPML**. You can also import from **From a file** in **Add a feed**.

- **Import OPML** — Bring in an `.opml` / `.xml` file exported from another reader.
  On a free account, keyword feeds are imported only up to your free limit (3, minus the ones you already have). The rest aren't imported (**[AURA Plus](/plus)** imports them all)
- **Export OPML** — Save your current feed list (you choose where to save it)

AURA also backs up your feed list on your device once a month (up to 6 months).

## Clean up feeds that can't load

If some feeds aren't loading, the top of the list shows “N sources couldn't be refreshed” (tap **Details** to see which ones and retry).
In **Manage feeds**:

- Tap **Can't load N** (with a ⚠ mark) at the top to open **Feeds that can't load**. There you can tap **Try N again** or **Unsubscribe from N at once**
- **⋮** > **Unsubscribe from N that can't load** unsubscribes from all of them at once, after you confirm
