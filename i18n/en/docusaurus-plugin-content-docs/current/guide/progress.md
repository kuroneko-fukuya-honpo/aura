# Refresh progress

During a refresh, a bar appears above the list (just below the tabs) and shows **what AURA is reading right now**.

- The chip on the left — **Fetch** (filled) means feeds are refreshing. **Full text** (outlined) means full text is being prefetched
- To its right — The folder name, and the source being read (Fetch) or the article headline (Full text)
- At the right edge — The current item / the total

## Full bar and mini bar

The bar has a full view (full bar) and a compact view (mini bar). The mini bar shows only a thin line and a “%.”

- **When you refresh yourself** (pull down on the list, tap **⋮** > **Refresh**, or right after adding a feed) — Starts as the full bar and collapses to the mini bar after 8 seconds
- **Auto-refresh** (scheduled) — Shows only the mini bar from the start

You can switch the bar with your finger.

- **Tap** — Full bar ⇄ mini bar. A full bar opened by tapping collapses again after 8 seconds
- **Touch and hold** — Full bar ⇄ mini bar. **A full bar opened by touching and holding stays open until you close it** (for a closer look)

The bar goes away when the refresh finishes.

## Three styles

In **Settings > Appearance > Refresh progress**, choose how the bar looks. A moving sample appears to the right of each one.

- **Simple (bar only)** — Just a thin bar. There's no full bar or mini bar
- **Scan ticker** (the default) — A light runs along the line
- **Pulse segments** — Separate segments light up in turn

## The color follows your accent color

The bar color changes to match the **accent color** you choose in [Theme and text](/guide/theme).

## New versions of the app

If you installed AURA from Google Play, “A new version is available” appears above the list when there's a new version.
Tap **Update** to download it in the background. When it's done, the message changes to “The update is ready.” Tap **Restart** to switch to the new version.

AURA checks Google Play at most once a day. To check right now, go to Settings > Info & support > **Check for updates**.
