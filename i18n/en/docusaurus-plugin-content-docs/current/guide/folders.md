# Folders

You can group feeds into folders. The tabs above the list are your folders. The drawer, which opens with **≡** at the top left, also lists feeds by folder (the folder you're viewing is shown expanded).

## Folder actions

**Touch and hold** a folder tab, or open the folder and tap **⋮** at the top right of the list > **Folder actions…**, to open a sheet of shortcuts for that folder. Under the title you see “N feeds · N unread.”

- **Photo frame** (AURA Plus) — Show photos from this folder
- **Stats** — AURA Brief for this folder
- **Mark all as read** — This folder only
- **Manage feeds** — Opens filtered to this folder
- **Name and order** — Opens Manage folders

When you're viewing a keyword feed, these become **Stats for this word** and **Settings for this feed**.

## Create, rename, and delete

In **Manage feeds** (list **⋮** > **Manage feeds**), tap **⋮** > **Manage folders**.

- Enter a name in **New folder name** and tap **Create**
- Tap **Rename** next to a folder to change its name
- Tap **Delete**. AURA asks “Delete “…”?” and reminds you that the feeds in it move to Uncategorized. Tap **Delete** to confirm
- Drag **≡** at the right edge to reorder folders

Deleting a folder **doesn't delete its feeds**. They move to Uncategorized.

## Uncategorized

Feeds you add without choosing a folder go here. **You can't rename or delete it.**
It always stays at the top of the drawer.

## Put feeds in folders and move them between folders

Each feed can be in only one folder. There are three ways to move one:

- **Manage feeds** — Touch and hold a row to pick it up. **Drop it past a folder heading to move it into that folder**
  (with **≡** at the right edge, you don't need to touch and hold). After moving, you see “Moved 1 to “…”.”
- **Drawer** — Touch and hold a feed and move your finger to reorder. **Drag it past a folder heading to move it into that folder**
  (you can drop it into a collapsed folder, too)
- **Settings sheet** (below) — Choose under **Folder**

In Manage feeds, you can reorder only when the search box at the top is empty and no filter is applied.

## Settings sheet (touch and hold)

**Touch and hold a feed and release without moving it** to open its settings sheet.
It's the same sheet in Manage feeds and in the drawer (in Manage feeds, tapping a row opens it too).

- **Rename**
- **Folder** — Choose a folder (you can also create one with **+ New folder**)
- **Show article images** — Turn off to hide list thumbnails and the image at the top of articles (images inside the article text stay)
- **Move to top** / **Open site**
- **Unsubscribe**

Folder headings don't have a touch-and-hold menu (touching and holding picks them up so you can reorder folders).

## Tab counts

The number under each tab is the article count for the view you've chosen (see [Read and save](/guide/read)).
Turn on Settings > Appearance > **Hide empty feeds and folders** to hide ones with no articles from tabs and menus.
