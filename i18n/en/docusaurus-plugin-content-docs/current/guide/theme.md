# Theme and text

Go to **Settings > Appearance** for the language, theme, and colors. Text settings are in **Settings > Read & listen**.

## Language

At the top of **Settings > Appearance**, choose **Language**: **Match the device** / **English** / **日本語**.
This changes the language of AURA's screens. Articles stay in their own language.

## Theme

Under **Display**, choose **Theme**.

![Settings > Appearance (theme and custom colors)](/img/guide/en/theme-settings.jpg)

- **Match device** / **Light** (the default) / **Dark** / **Black** (pure black for OLED screens)

## Custom colors

Turn on **Use custom colors** to choose two colors. While it's off, AURA uses the default **ink and vermilion** colors.

- **Primary color** — The top bar, tabs, and menu surfaces. Ink, Navy, Moss, Wine, Umber, Slate blue
- **Accent color** — Selection marks, counts, and buttons. Vermilion, Indigo, Forest, Plum, Amber, Slate

## Text

Go to **Settings > Read & listen > Text**.

![Settings > Read & listen > Text](/img/guide/en/theme-text.jpg)

- **Text size (overall)** — 80–160% (in 5% steps). As you move the slider, the sample below updates right away
- **Font** — The typeface for the list and for article headlines and text. You can try each one with a preview as you choose (**Mincho**, your device's serif font, is here too)
- **Title text size** — 12–22 sp. This changes only the size of headlines in the list

You can also change the text size with **aA** at the top right of the article screen.
