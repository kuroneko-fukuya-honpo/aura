# AURA roadmap (as of October 2026)

## In preparation

Nothing to announce right now (the Radipocket integration is now available: Settings > Connections > Radipocket).

## Under consideration

- AI summaries for each keyword or folder

- More natural article translation (right now, articles are handed to the translation app on your device)

- Finding articles about words you care about, even from outlets you don't subscribe to

- More features for AURA for G2
